# My Favorite Anime Series: Demon Slayer

Demon Slayer is one of my favorite anime series because of its beautiful animation and emotional story.  
It follows a boy named Tanjiro Kamado who becomes a demon slayer after his family is attacked by demons, and his sister Nezuko is turned into one.

## Main Characters
- Tanjiro Kamado  
- Nezuko Kamado  
- Zenitsu Agatsuma  
- Inosuke Hashibira  
- Kyojuro Rengoku

## Why I Like It
- Stunning animation and fight scenes  
- Strong sibling bond between Tanjiro and Nezuko  
- Inspiring story about courage and determination  

## Favorite Quote
> "Work at it. All I can do is work hard! That’s the story of my life." – Tanjiro Kamado
